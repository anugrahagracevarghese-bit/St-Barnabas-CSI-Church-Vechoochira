        St Barnabas CSI Church, located in Ennooramvayal, is a place of worship
        with a rich spiritual heritage and strong community bond. The church is located in Ranni, Pathanamthitta, Kerala nearby to the government office Panchayath Office, Vechoochira, as well as near Kulamamkuzhy St Andrew’s Marthoma church.<br>

<h2>📍 Basic details</h2>
<p>Location: Ennooramvayal, Vechoochira, Kerala</p>
<p>Denomination: Church of South India</p>
<p>Diocese: Madhya Kerala Diocese</p>
</p>Type: Parish church</p>
<br>
<h2>⛪ Growth of the parish</h2>
<p>Became an independent pastorate in 1958.<p>
<p>Today it has:<p/>
<p>Around 700 members</p>
<p>About 130+ families</p>
<p>Church property of over 2 acres</p>
<br>
<p>This shows steady growth from a small missionary outpost to a well-established parish.</p>
<br>
<h2>🏛️ History & origin</h2>
<p>The church was established in 1905.</p>
<p>It was founded by CMS (Church Missionary Society) missionaries, who played a major role in spreading Christianity and education in central Kerala.</p>
<p>The surrounding region (Vechoochira) itself developed during the early 1900s when missionary activity and migration increased.</p>
<br>
<h2>📌 Importance</h2>
<p>One of the early Protestant churches in the Vechoochira region</p>
<p>Represents the missionary expansion era (early 20th century) in Kerala</p>
<p>Continues to be an active spiritual center for the local community</p>

    <h2>Organizations</h2>
<hr>

<h1>📖Sunday School</h1>

<p><b>Mission:</b> The Sunday School operates with the goal of raising children in the path of God, helping them grow in faith and spiritual knowledge.</p>

<p><b>Curriculum and Structure:</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>Classes are conducted for children from age 3 to Grade 12.</li>
    <li>The school follows the syllabus and guidelines of the C.S.I. Madhya Kerala Diocese.</li>
    <li>Education is organized into Nursery, Primary, Junior, Intermediate, and Senior levels.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Activities:</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>Students participate in singing, Bible reading, quiz competitions, and other events at parish and district levels.</li>
    <li>Special programs such as Vacation Bible School (VBS) and Sunday School Sunday are regularly organized.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Leadership and Support:</b> The Sunday School is guided by a Headmaster/Headmistress along with a dedicated team of volunteer teachers who mentor and support the children spiritually and academically.</p>
<img src="ss.jpg" width="300" height="200" style="border-radius:10px";>


    <h1>🎶Church Choir</h1>
    <p><b>Mission:</b> The choir is dedicated to praising God through song, rooted in the belief expressed in Psalm 104:33:
    "I will sing to the Lord all my life; I will sing praise to my God as long as I live."</p>

    <p><b>Structure:</b> The group includes both senior and junior choirs, with new members joining through a dedication service held on Choir Sunday.</p>

    <p><b>Key Responsibilities:</b> They lead the congregation in hymns during Sunday worship, Christmas Carol services, and ecumenical gatherings.</p>

    <p><b>Training:</b> Regular practices are conducted to maintain a high standard of musical performance for worship services.</p>

    <p><b>Core Objectives:</b> It focuses on the holistic development of young people through worship, study, and service.</p>

    <p><b>Activities:</b> The group organizes spiritual and social programs including independent worship sessions, cricket competitions, and participation in diocesan-level youth meets.</p>

    <p><b>Social Impact:</b> Members are encouraged to participate in community activities and leadership training camps.</p>

    <p><b>Expansion:</b> The school recently completed a major infrastructure project with the construction of a new two-story building costing approximately 1 crore rupees, supported by church members and committees.</p>

    <p><b>Community and Academic Excellence:</b> Students regularly achieve scholarships and awards at the sub-district level. The school also has active PTA and MPTA groups supporting student welfare and development.</p>

function showTab(tabId) {

    let tabs = document.querySelectorAll('.tab');

    tabs.forEach(tab => {
        tab.classList.remove('active');
    });

    document.getElementById(tabId).classList.add('active');
}

function searchPage() {

    let input = document.getElementById("searchInput").value.toLowerCase();

    let tabs = document.querySelectorAll(".tab");

    let found = false;

    tabs.forEach(tab => {

        let elements = tab.querySelectorAll("h1, h2, h3, p, li");

        elements.forEach(element => {

            if(element.innerText.toLowerCase().includes(input) && !found){

                found = true;

                tabs.forEach(t => {
                    t.classList.remove("active");
                });

                tab.classList.add("active");

                setTimeout(() => {

                    element.scrollIntoView({
                        behavior: "smooth",
                        block: "center"
                    });

                    element.style.backgroundColor = "yellow";

                    setTimeout(() => {
                        element.style.backgroundColor = "";
                    }, 3000);

                }, 100);
            }
        });
    });

    if(!found){
        alert("No result found");
    }
}
</script>
